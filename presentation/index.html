Executive Presentation Strategy

Focusing the Father's attention on organizational outcomes and child engagement.

  • Traditional Challenges: Sunday School attendance logs on loose paper sheets are easily lost; paper handouts fail to engage students raised on highly gamified video-game interfaces.
  • The JoyfulPath Mission: To channel gaming mechanics (positive feedback loops) into spiritual education, making attendance, lessons, and tests interactive.
Pastor's Focus: Emphasize how this app acts as an administrative safety net—notifying servants if a student stops attending for multiple consecutive weeks.
⚡ Three Core Audiences

The platform binds the parish structure together:

  1. Students: Play quizzes, check-in, earn XP/points, and unlock profile themes.
  2. Servants: Scan check-ins in seconds, track student study statistics, and focus on mentorship.
  3. Parents: View attendance records, quiz grades, and progress on mobile in real-time.

Student Role & Interface

Vibrant, engaging dashboard designed for children to drive independent learning.

  • Interactive Dashboard: Shows current levels, XP tallies, coin points, and streak flames to motivate daily logins.
  • Weekly Bible Quizzes: Students answer questions on recent lectures to convert lessons into active knowledge and score XP.
  • Points Customization Shop: Redeeming earned coin points for avatar borders, profile colors, and biblical titles (e.g. "David's Warrior").
