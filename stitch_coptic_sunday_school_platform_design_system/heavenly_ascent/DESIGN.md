## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dignified, warm, and uplifting spiritual atmosphere tailored for a multi-generational community—embracing children, youth, servants (Khadem), parents, and clergy (Abouna). The visual philosophy merges contemporary digital product craftsmanship with illuminated Coptic heritage. 

The aesthetic is anchored in illuminated warmth, organic spiritual growth, and tranquil reverence. It eschews clinical enterprise patterns and harsh gamification tropes in favor of an inviting, sanctuary-like environment. The emotional experience balances quiet peace with joyful progress across the collective journey toward heaven. Visual metaphors center on the path, the lantern's light, living olive branches, and geometric Coptic cross motifs integrated with subtle restraint.

Key styling principles:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Strictly light-mode surfaces inspired by aged vellum, warm natural limestone, and linen. Pure black text and cold grays are replaced with deep cypress umber and warm taupe tones.
- **Dignified Modernity:** A crisp, accessible structure utilizing generous breathing room, soft structural cards, and golden sunlit ambiances.
- **Harmonious Gamification:** Rewarding devotion, study, and attendance through gentle tokens resembling sacred gems, warm honey light, and verdant growth rather than flashing neon badges.

## Typography

The typography structure is engineered specifically for RTL Arabic with full Latin companion parity using the clean, dignified forms of IBM Plex Sans (leveraging IBM Plex Sans Arabic). This creates a balanced voice that is respectful and authoritative for patristic texts, scriptures, and liturgical hymns, while remaining fluid and legible for children and youth on responsive screens.

Rules for implementation:
- **Leading & Generosity:** Arabic typography requires greater vertical breathing room; line-height tokens maintain an expanded factor (1.55x to 1.7x on body text) to accommodate diacritics (tashkeel) without clipping.
- **Weight Distribution:** Display and headline levels leverage Bold (`700`) and SemiBold (`600`) for clear hierarchy. Body text uses Regular (`400`) to maximize scan-ability across long biblical readings and commentary.
- **Directional Fluidity:** Default text alignment aligns to the right (`dir="rtl"`). Numerical values, liturgical hymn timestamps, and streak counters maintain contextual digit clarity with proportional numerals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a strict 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for tight status badges, progress bars, and metadata chips. Content adapts predictably between broad servant/admin dashboards and mobile-first student/parent portals.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px margins. Maximum content boundary capped at 1280px for standard reading and 1440px for servant administrative data grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Side navigation contracts to an icon-and-label rail.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px lateral padding. Bottom floating navigation bar handles primary student/parent routes with elevated reach zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting rather than cool drop shadows or harsh outline borders. Depth mimics soft natural light falling across stacked vellum parchment.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background layer (`#FAF6EE`), flat with no drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Standard Panels):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate boundary of `1px solid #EFE8DC` and an ambient sunlit shadow: `0px 2px 10px -1px rgba(180, 140, 75, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Elevated Modules):** Used on active lesson cards, streak highlights, and servant attendance toggles: `0px 6px 20px -2px rgba(180, 140, 75, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns):** Sacred notices, scripture modals, and prize claim dialogues: `0px 12px 36px -4px rgba(43, 30, 10, 0.12)`.
- **Liturgical Accent (Spiritual Moments):** Reserved for star achievements, prayers, and milestones: soft golden aura glow `0px 0px 24px 0px rgba(201, 147, 59, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, hospitable curves that communicate gentleness and welcome without feeling juvenile or unserious.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Inputs, tooltips, list row states, and inline table filters.
- **Intermediate (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Primary UI cards, task items, attendance roster entries, and gamification challenge modules.
- **Expansive (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Hero banners, scripture journey cards, celebration cards, and parent overview modules.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status badges, streak counters, avatar wrappers, action chips, and primary floating action buttons.

### Cards & Liturgical Modules
- Pure white container with `1px solid #EFE8DC`, resting on the warm parchment canvas.
- Subtle gold header accent border (`2px solid #C9933B`) on prayer cards, scripture verses, and weekly servant agendas.
- Inner padding strictly adhering to `space-md` (16px) for compact feeds and `space-lg` (24px) for prominent content sections.

### Chips & Gamification Badges
- **Streak Tracker:** Pill-shaped, warm coral background (`#EA580C` at 10% opacity), solid `#EA580C` text, paired with a fire/lamp icon.
- **XP / Points:** Burnished gold background (`#D97706` at 12% opacity), text `#B45309`, containing bold numerical indicator.
- **Attendance Status:**
  - *Present:* Soft mint background (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#15803D`), circular green glyph.
  - *Excused / Pending:* Soft amber background (`#FFFBEB`), warm ochre text (`#B45309`).
  - *Absent:* Soft rose background (`#FEF2F2`), ruby text (`#DC2626`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Clean white container, `1.5px` border in `#EFE8DC`, placeholder `#8C9B92`. Focus ring transitions to `#165B4C` with a 3px ambient ring of `rgba(22, 91, 76, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded square (checkbox: 6px radius) and circle (radio). Default unselected border `#C7BCAB`. Selected state filled with primary forest green (`#165B4C`) and pure white indicator.

### Lists & Rosters (Servant / Abouna Views)
- Alternating subtle rows using `#FAF6EE` on hover. Divider lines styled as fine warm sand `#EFE8DC`.
- Each student item features an integrated attendance toggle, point counter, and parental contact quick-action.

### Special Spiritual Elements

- **Journey Progress Bar:** Layered track in `#EFE8DC` with active gradient fill advancing from `#165B4C` through `#C9933B` with illuminated star nodes indicating milestones.
